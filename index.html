RunIt

01 · The main project
Live on the App Store and Google Play

Your everyday runs become an adventure.

RunIt takes the kilometers you already log and moves you along a route on a map: the length of Sweden, around Taiwan, along the Great Wall of China or across the Bering Strait. Friends join the same adventure and move together, milestones pop up with a photo and a short story, and a league lets a group race week by week.

Workouts arrive automatically from Apple Health and Health Connect, so an Apple Watch, Garmin or Polar all count. Only real, logged workouts move you forward. No manual entry, no cheating.

I built it alone, from the first database table to App Store review, while studying full time. Designed in Swedish for my own friends first, and they are still the most active users.

05

A character and a wardrobe

Everyone is a little figure on the map. Outfits are earned by running, never bought, and seasonal passes add levels to unlock.

06

Built to spread in group chats

Every adventure link renders its own share card with the route and progress. Invite links, push notifications and email digests keep the group moving.

How it's built

One Next.js app on Vercel does everything: the web app, the API and the pages people share. Capacitor wraps the same code for the App Store and Google Play, and two small native plugins in Swift and Kotlin read Apple Health and Health Connect.

Prisma and a PostgreSQL database hold adventures, workouts, leagues and milestones. Push notifications, transactional email, Open Graph image generation, an admin panel with usage analytics and a seasonal pass system are all part of the same codebase.

I also wrote the maps, the milestone stories and the App Store listing, handled Apple's review three times over, and measure retention with my own scripts against production.
